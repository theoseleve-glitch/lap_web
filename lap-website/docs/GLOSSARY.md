# Telemetry Glossary

Canonical definitions for telemetry terms used across LAP marketing and product copy. Reference this when writing — do not invent synonyms.

| Term | Definition | Use this phrasing | Avoid |
|------|-----------|-------------------|-------|
| **Delta** | Time difference between two laps at a given point on track | "delta to reference lap" or "time delta" | "gap", "offset", "time gap" |
| **Apex** | The innermost point of the racing line through a corner | "corner apex" or "apex point" | "clipping point", "turning point" |
| **Braking point** | Where the driver begins braking before a corner | "braking point" or "brake marker" | "brake zone start", "stopping point" |
| **Turn-in** | The point where the driver initiates steering into a corner | "turn-in point" | "entry point", "steering initiation" |
| **Minimum speed** | The lowest speed recorded through a corner | "minimum corner speed" or "corner min-speed" | "slowest speed", "nadir" |
| **Throttle pickup** | The point where the driver begins applying throttle exiting a corner | "throttle pickup point" | "gas application", "power-on" |
| **Trail braking** | Technique of maintaining brake pressure into the corner entry while turning | "trail braking" | "brake-and-turn", "late braking" (different concept) |
| **Grip utilisation** | Percentage of available tyre grip being used (from G-G diagram) | "grip utilisation" | "traction usage", "friction use" |
| **Racing line** | The optimal path through a corner or series of corners | "racing line" or "ideal line" | "trajectory", "track line" |
| **Sector** | A defined segment of the track used for timing comparison | "sector" or "track sector" | "segment", "section", "zone" |
| **G-G diagram** | Scatter plot of lateral vs longitudinal acceleration showing tyre usage | "G-G diagram" or "friction circle" | "traction circle", "g-force plot" |
| **Corner phase** | One of entry, apex, or exit phases within a single corner | "corner phase" | "turn segment", "corner part" |
| **Lap time** | Total elapsed time for one complete circuit | "lap time" | "lap duration", "circuit time" |
| **Session** | A single continuous recording of telemetry data on track | "session" | "run", "stint" (stint implies endurance context) |
| **Coaching report** | The automated analysis output produced by LAP | "coaching report" | "telemetry report", "analysis report" |
