# LAP Glossary

Canonical definitions for telemetry terms used across the product and marketing site. Use these exact phrasings in copy. Avoid the listed alternatives.

| Term | Definition | Use This Phrasing | Avoid |
|------|-----------|-------------------|-------|
| **Delta** | The time difference between two laps or two drivers at a given point on track. | "pace delta", "time delta" | "gap", "difference", "offset" |
| **Apex** | The geometric or optimal closest point to the inside of a corner. | "corner apex", "apex speed" | "clipping point", "turn vertex" |
| **Braking point** | The track position where the driver begins to brake before a corner. | "braking point", "brake marker" | "brake zone start", "initial braking" |
| **Turn-in** | The moment the driver begins steering into the corner. | "turn-in point" | "corner entry start", "steering initiation" |
| **Minimum speed** | The lowest speed reached during a corner, typically near the apex. | "minimum corner speed", "min speed" | "lowest velocity", "bottom speed" |
| **Throttle pickup** | The point after the apex where the driver begins to reapply throttle. | "throttle pickup", "throttle application point" | "gas on", "power application" |
| **Trail braking** | The technique of maintaining light braking while turning into a corner. | "trail braking", "trail-brake phase" | "braking while turning", "late braking" |
| **Grip utilization** | The percentage of available tyre grip being used, combining lateral and longitudinal forces. | "grip utilization", "tyre usage" | "grip level", "friction circle usage" |
| **Racing line** | The optimal path through a corner or series of corners that minimizes lap time. | "racing line", "optimal line" | "ideal path", "fastest route" |
| **Sector** | A defined segment of a lap, used to break down performance into measurable parts. | "sector", "track sector" | "segment", "section", "zone" |
| **Stint** | A continuous period of driving between pit stops. | "stint", "driving stint" | "run", "session portion" |
| **G-G diagram** | A plot of lateral vs. longitudinal acceleration, showing how close the driver gets to the vehicle's grip limit. | "G-G diagram", "friction circle" | "G-force plot", "acceleration map" |
| **Pace** | The overall speed of a driver measured by lap times. One of the four scoring pillars. | "pace", "pace score" | "speed", "quickness" |
| **Consistency** | How repeatable a driver's lap times are. One of the four scoring pillars. | "consistency", "consistency score" | "repeatability", "steadiness" |
| **Execution** | How well a driver hits optimal references (braking, apex, throttle). One of the four scoring pillars. | "execution", "execution score" | "technique", "precision" |
| **Robustness** | How well a driver maintains performance under pressure or changing conditions. One of the four scoring pillars. | "robustness", "robustness score" | "resilience", "durability" |
