Data processors
Third-party providers used by the platform include:

User rights (GDPR)
Users have the right to:

correct inaccurate data

request data portability

legal@lap.coach

Data security
Lap Coach implements technical and organizational measures designed to protect user data against unauthorized access or disclosure.

Complaints
Users may file complaints with the French data protection authority:
CNIL – Commission Nationale de l’Informatique et des Libertés.
