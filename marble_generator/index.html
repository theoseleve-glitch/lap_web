Element Contact

Move your mouse here. The brush doubles in size near UI elements, smearing the marble beneath as you move.

Scroll Parallax

Scroll the page. The marble shifts at 30% speed. Mouse movement pushes the marble as you scroll through it.

Permanent Deformation

Mouse movement permanently smears the marble. Every motion pushes pixels along your path. Hit Reset to restore.

Keep scrolling to see the marble parallax effect. The background shifts at 30% of scroll speed, creating a layered depth illusion.

The marble texture is a static image rendered once from noise. All interactions (mouse, scroll, element hover) use GPU-accelerated canvas compositing — no per-pixel work at 60fps.

This component can be embedded in any frontend. The canvas sits at z-index 0 with pointer-events: none. Content floats above.
